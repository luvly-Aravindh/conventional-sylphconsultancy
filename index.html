A few things worth clearing up

Before you check.

Can the feature be timed to our launch?

Yes. Tell us your launch window. We publish four editions a month and plan launches, anniversaries and other milestones to the edition. The earlier we know, the closer to your launch it runs.

Who reads Sylph?

Sylph publishes four editions a month, 48 a year: 60,000 to 70,000 copies, hand-delivered to UHNI and HNI homes on Sylph’s own list, and into boardrooms, embassies and estates. More than 80,000 UHNIs and HNIs read it, in India and across Dubai, London, New York and Singapore.

Do you work with the founder or the marketing team?

Whoever leads the project. Founder profiles are written with the founder. Sylph works with founders and senior leadership at India’s leading developers, including Signature Global.

How much does it cost?

Every placement has one fixed price, from a single feature to a full year. You see yours at the end of the check, so the call is about the plan, not the price.

Do you make coffee table books?

Yes. A magazine carries many names in one edition. A coffee table book carries one: yours, on every page, on premium paper and in premium packaging. Sylph Consultancy produces them, as its Legacy Book™ series, for developers, architects and brands. There is also the custom edition, like Signature Global’s 60-page collector’s edition: a Sylph edition with your project on the cover, printed in a dedicated run of 1,000 copies or more for your own buyers. Ask about either on the call.

Do you accept renders?

Yes, held to a strict standard, and always labelled as renders in print, so readers are never misled. When photographs fall short, our editors ask for a re-shoot or choose the frames that work.

Is it an advert?

A feature is an article, written by Sylph’s editors. The annual programme can also include adverts, planned alongside the features.

We already have an agency.

Keep them. Sylph is not an ad agency. We write and publish, and your agency can build the launch around it.

What is guaranteed?

Publishing. Every page in your package is published.
